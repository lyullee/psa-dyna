# References and parameter provenance

No article PDF or publisher figure is redistributed in this repository.

1. Wang et al. (2023), hydrogen-rich multicomponent adsorption and VPSA study,
   *Chemical Engineering Journal* 472, 144920.
   [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cej.2023.144920](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cej.2023.144920)
   
   Used for the RB3 apparatus geometry, adsorption parameters and the
   figure-level single-bed breakthrough benchmark. The single-bed result is
   not presented as cyclic VPSA validation.

2. Ahn et al. (2012), multicomponent adsorption parameters for layered
   activated carbon/zeolite 5A hydrogen purification.
   [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ces.2011.09.053](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ces.2011.09.053)

3. Corrigendum to the Ahn parameter table.
   [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ces.2013.06.043](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ces.2013.06.043)

   The corrigendum identifies the heat unit as cal/mol. PSA-Dyna converts the
   independently tabulated heat values using 4.184 J/cal while preserving the
   published affinity-temperature convention.

4. Marcinek et al. (2021), dynamic high-purity nitrogen PSA model.
   [https://doi.org/10.1007/s10450-021-00320-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10450-021-00320-0)

5. Associated open workbook record for the Marcinek/Guderian adapter.
   [https://doi.org/10.17632/d2vx7w6x74.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/d2vx7w6x74.1)

6. Dantas et al. (2011), fixed-bed CO2 adsorption benchmark.
   [https://doi.org/10.1590/S0104-66322011000300018](https://doi.org/10.1590/S0104-66322011000300018)

7. Yavary et al. (2016), quaternary H2/CO2/CH4/CO competitive equilibrium
   on commercial zeolite 5A over 1--18 bar and 298.15--318.15 K.
   [https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jced.6b00312](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jced.6b00312)

8. Chen, Ritter and Yang (1990), nonideal multicomponent adsorption on 5A
   molecular sieve at elevated pressure and temperature.
   [https://doi.org/10.1016/0009-2509(90)80179-I](https://doi.org/10.1016/0009-2509(90)80179-I)

9. Chan and Lawther (1967), radial bed-to-wall heat transfer in a packed bed.
   [ANSTO repository record](https://apo.ansto.gov.au/items/aeb3b687-aaa7-76a5-e053-150a9d89ded9)

10. Freiwald and Paterson (1992), accuracy of model predictions and
    reliability of experimental packed-bed heat-transfer data.
    [https://doi.org/10.1016/0009-2509(92)85003-T](https://doi.org/10.1016/0009-2509(92)85003-T)

11. Radial heat transport in packed beds at elevated pressures (1992),
    including separate effective radial-conductivity and wall-transfer
    measurements.
    [https://doi.org/10.1016/0255-2701(92)80011-Q](https://doi.org/10.1016/0255-2701(92)80011-Q)

References are assigned per adapter. Parameters from different papers,
adsorbent grades or gas systems are not pooled into one calibration.
